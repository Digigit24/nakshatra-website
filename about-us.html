Our Story

Welcome to Nakshatra IVF

Pregnancy and childbirth are milestones in a woman’s life. To make this journey pleasant, enjoyable and safe, Nakshatra clinic, one of the best infertility clinics in Baner, Hinjewadi, Wakad, Ravet & Baner, provides complete care to new mothers and mothers-to-be. We offer treatments to couples who are facing difficulty with conceiving.

We offer the most advanced treatment, including gynecology, Obstetrics, Fertility (both men and women), Cosmetic gynecology, sexual medicine  etc. Headed by highly qualified IVF specialist Dr Ramit Kamate, we are the most reputable and best infertility clinic in Baner, Hinjewadi, Wakad, Ravet & Baner.
