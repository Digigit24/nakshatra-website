Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is IVF and how does it work?

IVF (In Vitro Fertilization) is a process where eggs and sperm are combined outside the body in a laboratory to create embryos. Once the embryos are formed, they are transferred into the uterus to achieve pregnancy. IVF is often recommended for couples with fertility challenges such as blocked fallopian tubes, male infertility, or unexplained infertility.

2. What are the main causes of infertility in men and women?

Women: Age-related fertility decline, ovulation disorders, blocked fallopian tubes, endometriosis, or hormonal imbalances.
Men: Low sperm count, poor sperm motility, abnormal sperm morphology, or genetic issues. Understanding these causes helps fertility specialists choose the most suitable treatment, like IVF, ICSI, or IUI.

3. What is the difference between IVF, ICSI, and IUI?

IVF: Eggs are fertilized by sperm in a lab, and embryos are transferred to the uterus.
ICSI: A single sperm is injected directly into an egg; often used for male infertility.
IUI: Sperm is placed directly into the uterus during ovulation; less invasive than IVF.

4. How does age affect IVF success?

Age is the most critical factor in IVF success. Women under 35 generally have the highest success rates (40-55%), while success gradually decreases after 35. Clinics often recommend egg freezing or donor programs for women over 40 to improve chances.

5. What lifestyle factors can improve IVF outcomes?

Lifestyle changes can enhance fertility and IVF success:

  • Maintain a healthy weight and diet
  • Avoid smoking and alcohol
  • Manage stress with yoga or meditation
  • Regular moderate exercise
  • Follow doctor-prescribed supplements and medications

6. Can IVF help with male infertility?

Yes. IVF, often combined with ICSI, is highly effective for male infertility issues such as low sperm count, poor sperm motility, or abnormal sperm morphology. Leading Pune clinics have specialized embryologists to handle such cases.

7. What is Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT) in IVF?

PGT is a genetic screening method to detect chromosomal abnormalities in embryos before implantation. It is recommended for couples with:

  • Recurrent miscarriages
  • Advanced maternal age
  • Family history of genetic disorders
PGT improves the chance of healthy pregnancy and reduces miscarriage risk.
