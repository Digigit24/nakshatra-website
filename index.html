Milestone 1
Baner, Pune

Every journey begins with a single cell.

Infertility is a deeply personal concern among couples of various ages, often bringing emotional stress and lowered confidence. At Nakshatra Clinic, we believe that parenthood is a dream worth pursuing—and with the right medical support, it is absolutely achievable. In Vitro Fertilisation (IVF) is a carefully structured path designed to help individuals or couples safely conceive and experience the pure joy of parenthood.

Milestone 2
Advanced Precision Embryology

ICSI: Overcoming Male Infertility Barriers

For couples navigating challenges like low sperm count, poor motility, or unexplained infertility, our specialists implement ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection). Under expert embryologist supervision, a single healthy sperm is selected with micro-fine precision and injected directly into the egg, vastly increasing the absolute probability of successful fertilization.

Milestone 3
Modern Reproductive Tech

Blastocyst Culture & ICM Selection

We grow embryos for 5 to 6 days until they reach the advanced Blastocyst stage. Utilizing specialized Inner Cell Mass (ICM) analysis techniques, we closely evaluate cellular health and structural viability. By transferring only the healthiest, most resilient embryos, we substantially improve implantation outcomes while lowering pregnancy risks.

Milestone 4
Optimized Outcomes

Frozen Embryo Transfer & Success

Timing is everything. Frozen Embryo Transfer allows preserved embryos from previous cycles to be safely introduced when your body is naturally resting and at its absolute physical peak. By combining personalized treatment plans, compassionate care, and evidence-based protocols, Nakshatra Clinic achieves a powerful 50% to 70% average IVF success rate per cycle.
