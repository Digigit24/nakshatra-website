Fertility Testing & Diagnosis

Accurate treatment starts with an accurate diagnosis. We provide comprehensive fertility testing for both partners — from hormone analysis to semen testing — to design a tailored treatment plan just for you.

Ovulation Monitoring

Timing is critical in IUI. We closely monitor your ovulation cycle using ultrasound imaging and hormone tracking to identify the ideal window for the procedure, improving success rates significantly.

Sperm Preparation

We carefully prepare the sperm sample in our laboratory, selecting the healthiest and most motile sperm. This vital step significantly enhances success rates and ensures only the best-quality sperm is used.

IUI Procedure

The IUI procedure is quick, simple, and generally painless. Our specialists gently place prepared sperm directly into the uterus at the optimal time. The process takes just a few minutes and you can return home the same day.

Medication & Hormonal Support

Step-by-Step IUI Procedure: What to Expect

Starting fertility treatment can feel overwhelming — but knowing what to expect makes the journey much easier. At our IUI center in Hinjewadi, Pune, we guide you through every step with care, clarity, and complete support.

Step 1

Initial Consultation

Your journey begins with a friendly consultation where our fertility expert listens to your concerns and reviews your medical history. Basic diagnostic tests may be recommended to identify the underlying cause and create a personalised treatment plan.

Step 2

Ovulation Monitoring

We carefully track your ovulation cycle through ultrasound scans and, if needed, ovulation-inducing medications. This step is critical because precise timing plays a major role in treatment success.

Book Consultation

Step 3

Sperm Preparation

On the day of the procedure, a sperm sample is collected and processed in our laboratory. The healthiest and most active sperm are selected to maximise the chances of successful fertilisation.

Step 4

IUI Procedure

A thin, soft catheter is used to place the prepared sperm directly into the uterus. The procedure takes only a few minutes and is generally well-tolerated with minimal discomfort.

Know More

Step 5

Post-Treatment Care

After the procedure, you can return to your normal routine with minimal restrictions. Our team will advise you on what to expect, when to take a pregnancy test, and provide emotional support during the two-week waiting period.
