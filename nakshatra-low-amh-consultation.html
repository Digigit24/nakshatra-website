Understanding Your Report

"Does low AMH mean I can't get pregnant naturally?"

No. And most doctors don't have time to explain why. Here is the full picture.

AMH tells you
  • How many follicles remain (ovarian reserve)
  • How your ovaries may respond to IVF stimulation drugs
  • A rough picture of how many eggs are left
  • One data point among many
AMH does NOT tell you
  • Whether those eggs are good quality
  • Whether you can conceive naturally this cycle
  • Your hormone balance, thyroid health, or nutritional status
  • Your complete fertility picture
A 2019 study in JAMA found no significant difference in natural pregnancy rates between women with low AMH and normal AMH in women under 40 without a diagnosed infertility condition. You were given a number. Nobody explained what it means for you.
The Full Picture

What Your AMH Number Actually Means

In plain language, with no medical jargon.

AMH Level
What It Means
What It Does NOT Mean
Low (< 1.0 ng/mL)
Fewer follicles available per cycle
That natural pregnancy is impossible
Borderline (1.0-1.5 ng/mL)
Reduced but still present ovarian reserve
That you are out of options naturally
Normal (1.5-3.5 ng/mL)
Typical reserve for age
Guaranteed conception without support
Think of it this way: AMH is like checking how much fuel is left in the tank. It tells you quantity, not whether the engine runs well, whether the road is clear, or whether you'll reach your destination. It is one number, not a verdict.
What Actually Matters

What's In Your Control and What Isn't

Natural conception depends on several factors. Some are fixed. Most are not.

Not in your control
  • Your age
  • Your AMH number
  • Number of follicles remaining
Very much in your control
  • Egg quality & mitochondrial health
  • Vitamin D & nutritional deficiencies
  • Hormonal imbalances (thyroid, FSH)
  • Oxidative stress on your eggs
  • Ovulation timing & cycle tracking
  • Cortisol & stress load on your cycle
  • The right supplements at the right dose
90 days

An egg takes approximately 90 days to mature before ovulation. During those 90 days, the environment around that egg - nutrition, hormones, stress, sleep - directly influences its quality. This is the window. This is what a proper consultation works with.
