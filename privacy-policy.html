Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

At Nakshatra IVF Clinic (accessible from https://www.nakshatraclinic.com or associated application APIs), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected, recorded, and handled by our systems and how we utilize it to coordinate your fertility treatment journey.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at nakshtraclinic@gmail.com.

1. Consent

By using our website, submitting an appointment registration form, or interacting with our integrated mobile/social applications (such as Meta Facebook Login/API features), you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

2. Information We Collect

The personal information that you are asked to provide, and the reasons why you are asked to provide it, will be made clear to you at the point we ask you to provide it.

  • Direct Form Submissions: When you book a consultation using our online appointment form, we collect your Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Phone Number, preferred fertility service, and preferred date of appointment.
  • Meta/Facebook Social API Data: If you connect with our platforms using Meta Facebook Auth, Login, or associated developer integrations, we only retrieve your standard, authorized public profile details (Name, Profile ID, and authorized email address) as permitted by your Facebook permissions grid to authenticate your clinic dashboard session.
  • Log Files & Analytics: Like standard web platforms, we utilize Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, and Meta (Facebook) Pixel. These tools collect automated diagnostic details such as IP addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISPs), referring/exit page redirects, and event stamps. This data is entirely aggregated and is never personally identifiable.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Schedule, organize, and confirm your in-clinic consultations.
  • Provide, operate, and maintain our online booking services.
  • Improve, personalize, and optimize your navigation experience on our site.
  • Understand and analyze how you interact with our website to optimize patient service flows.
  • Communicate directly with you to send appointment reminders, treatment schedules, and administrative alerts.
  • Detect, prevent, and mitigate diagnostic software errors or suspicious bot behaviors.

Data Deletion Instructions & Meta compliance

In accordance with Meta Platform Policy and global privacy standards, we strictly respect your right to manage, retrieve, and delete your personal data. If you have connected with our platform via Facebook developer integrations and wish to request the absolute deletion of your personal data, you can do so easily:

  • Send an email request to us at nakshtraclinic@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  • Provide your registered name, email address, or Facebook Profile ID in the email body.
  • Our technical operations team will verify your account, process the deletion request, and permanently wipe your personal details from our active databases and servers within 48 to 72 business hours.
  • A confirmation email will be sent to you as soon as the deletion has been successfully finalized.

4. Information Security & Protection

We implement industry-standard secure socket layers (SSL) and administrative encryption guards to safeguard your details against unauthorized access, alterations, disclosure, or destruction. However, please be aware that no transmission method over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute physical safety.

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Nakshatra IVF Clinic's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or external websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party services (such as Google Ads or Meta Ads platforms) for more detailed information.

6. CCPA & GDPR Privacy Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access – You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification – You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure – You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing – You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability – You have the right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you.
