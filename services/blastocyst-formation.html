Culture & Transfer Process

Blastocyst culture & transfer: how it works

Understand the key stages involved in blastocyst culture and embryo transfer during IVF treatment.

1

Egg Retrieval & Fertilisation

Mature eggs are collected and fertilised via IVF/ICSI under expert supervision.

2

Embryo Culture

Embryos are grown in state-of-the-art incubators designed to mimic natural conditions.

3

Monitoring & Grading

Cell division and morphology are checked to identify stronger-performing embryos.

4

Blastocyst Selection

Day 5/6 embryos are selected for fresh transfer or vitrification (FET).

5

Embryo Transfer

A gentle, ultrasound-guided transfer of a single high-quality blastocyst is usually performed to reduce multiple pregnancy risk while supporting treatment goals.

Blastocyst formation in IVF occurs when a fertilized egg develops for about five days into a mature stage called a blastocyst. This stage has more cells and better implantation potential, increasing the IVF success rate. At Nakshatra Clinic Baner, Pune specialists monitor this growth closely to select the healthiest embryos for transfer.

Blastocyst culture allows embryos to grow longer in a controlled lab setting, helping embryologists identify the most viable ones for transfer. This improves implantation chances and reduces multiple pregnancy risks. Fertility clinics in Pune use this technique to enhance IVF outcomes and ensure healthier pregnancies.

During a day-5 blastocyst transfer, a mature blastocyst is placed directly into the uterus using a thin catheter. This precise procedure, performed at leading fertility clinics in Baner, Pune, aligns with the natural timing of embryo implantation, improving the chances of a successful pregnancy.
