Overview

Minimally invasive view inside the uterus

Hysteroscopy is a minimally invasive diagnostic and therapeutic procedure used to examine the inside of the uterus with a thin, lighted instrument called a hysteroscope. This advanced gynaecological technique helps specialists visualise the uterine cavity, endometrium, and cervical canal to identify and treat problems like abnormal uterine bleeding, fibroids, or polyps.

At our advanced fertility and gynaecology centre in Baner, Pune, experienced specialists perform both diagnostic hysteroscopy and operative hysteroscopy with precision and care.

Conditions Treated

Conditions treated with hysteroscopy

Common conditions that may be evaluated or treated through hysteroscopy.

Abnormal Uterine Bleeding

Evaluates heavy or irregular periods and enables targeted treatment of fibroids, polyps, or lining issues.

Endometrial Polyps

Removes small growths that can cause spotting or infertility, without major surgery.

Uterine Fibroids

Minimally invasive removal of submucosal fibroids to ease heavy bleeding, pain, and infertility concerns.

Intrauterine Adhesions

Gently releases scar tissue to restore the uterine cavity and support periods and fertility after surgery or infection.

Uterine Septum / Structural Issues

Corrects congenital septum and cavity irregularities to create a healthier site for implantation.

Retained Products of Conception

Safely removes retained tissue after miscarriage or delivery to help prevent infection, bleeding, and future complications.

Foreign Bodies / IUD Issues

Locates and removes misplaced or embedded IUDs precisely, reducing pain and infection risk.

Endometrial Growths / Thickening

Allows direct biopsy and treatment of abnormal lining changes to support early detection and uterine health.

Postmenopausal Bleeding

Visualises the cavity to diagnose polyps, hyperplasia, or cancer and guide timely treatment.

Infertility Evaluation

Detects hidden uterine factors such as polyps, adhesions, or septum that may affect implantation.

Procedure Types

Types of hysteroscopy procedures

Operative Hysteroscopy

  • Treats intrauterine adhesions
  • Corrects congenital uterine anomalies
