Overview

Specialized support for fertilization challenges

Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI) is a specialized fertility treatment that has transformed possibilities for couples facing male infertility challenges. At Nakshatra Clinic in Baner, Pune, IVF specialists provide personalized care and advanced ICSI procedures for cases where traditional IVF may not be effective.

ICSI involves the precise injection of a single sperm into the cytoplasm of a mature egg in a laboratory setting, helping fertilization when sperm count, motility, or shape is compromised. This makes it an important treatment option for severe male-factor infertility.

Advantages

Advantages of ICSI treatment

High Fertilization Success

ICSI directly injects sperm into the egg, overcoming low sperm count, poor motility, or abnormal shape, and improving fertilization in severe male infertility cases.

Solution for Failed IVF

Couples who experienced unsuccessful IVF cycles may benefit from ICSI because it provides an alternative way to fertilize eggs.

Works with Frozen or Limited Eggs

ICSI is useful when few eggs are available or when frozen or in vitro matured eggs need careful fertilization support.

Treats Multiple Male Infertility Issues

ICSI addresses low sperm count, poor motility, and abnormal sperm morphology as part of male-factor infertility care.

Predictive DNA Assessment

When combined with sperm DNA fragmentation tests, ICSI planning can include sperm quality evaluation to guide fertility treatment decisions.
