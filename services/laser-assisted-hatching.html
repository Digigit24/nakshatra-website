Overview

Helping selected embryos hatch before implantation

Every couple dreams of holding their little miracle, and Laser Assisted Hatching (LAH) is designed to improve embryo implantation support during IVF in selected cases. This gentle fertility technique may be especially useful for couples with previous IVF failures or specific embryo-related challenges. At Nakshatra Clinic, the procedure combines advanced laser technology with careful embryology lab handling and compassionate fertility care.

Who May Benefit

When laser hatching may be discussed

Suitability is individualized by the fertility team after reviewing IVF history and embryo factors.

Previous IVF Failures

Couples with repeated implantation difficulty may be assessed for hatching support.

Age Over 35

Some women over 35 may be considered for LAH based on embryo and IVF history.

Thick Zona Pellucida

Embryos with a thicker outer shell may need additional lab evaluation.

Frozen-Thawed Embryos

Selected frozen-thawed embryos may be reviewed for assisted hatching when appropriate.

Poor Implantation History

LAH may be discussed where embryo implantation has been a repeated concern.

Individualized IVF Planning

The decision is guided by fertility history, embryo quality, and specialist recommendation.
