Overview

Low AMH does not mean motherhood is impossible

Being told you have low AMH levels can feel overwhelming. Low AMH indicates that ovarian reserve may be lower than average, but it does not automatically end the possibility of pregnancy. At Nakshatra Clinic, women receive low AMH treatment in Baner, Pune with advanced diagnostics, IVF planning where suitable, and patient-centred support to take confident steps toward parenthood.
