Overview

Advanced sperm retrieval for severe male infertility

Microsurgical Testicular Sperm Extraction, or Micro-TESE, is an advanced surgical procedure used to retrieve sperm directly from the testicles. It is primarily recommended for men with Non-Obstructive Azoospermia, where sperm is absent in the ejaculate. The procedure uses high-precision microscopic technology to identify areas more likely to contain sperm while aiming to limit testicular tissue damage.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Micro-TESE is a microsurgical procedure that retrieves sperm directly from the testes, commonly considered for men with non-obstructive azoospermia.

Micro-TESE uses a high-power operating microscope to identify sperm-rich tubules more precisely than standard TESE.

It is performed under anesthesia by specialists. The team explains risks, recovery instructions, and follow-up needs before the procedure.

Micro-TESE can help locate sperm in selected severe male infertility cases while using magnification to reduce unnecessary tissue removal.

Potential risks include bleeding, infection, discomfort, difficulty retrieving sperm, rare testicular damage, and anesthesia-related complications.
