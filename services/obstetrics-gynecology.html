FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Regular gynecological check-ups help detect early signs of uterine fibroids, hormonal imbalances, infections, or cervical abnormalities. At Nakshatra Clinic, specialists support preventive screening, early diagnosis, and personalized treatment for long-term reproductive and hormonal health.

Women aged 21 to 65 are commonly advised to get a Pap smear every three years to screen for cervical cancer. If risk factors or family history are present, the gynecologist may recommend more frequent testing.

Abnormal uterine bleeding may result from hormonal imbalance, fibroids, thyroid disorders, or stress. Diagnosis may include ultrasound, hormonal evaluation, or hysteroscopy, followed by a suitable treatment plan.

Hysteroscopy allows doctors to view and treat uterine problems like fibroids, polyps, or abnormal bleeding through a minimally invasive approach.

High-risk pregnancy care is needed when conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, thyroid disorders, or multiple pregnancies are present. It involves closer monitoring and personalized pregnancy support.

Maintaining hygiene, drinking enough water, and wearing breathable cotton underwear may help. If itching, discharge, burning, or pain occurs, a gynecology consultation helps with diagnosis and treatment.

Consult a gynecologist for irregular periods, pelvic pain, heavy bleeding, vaginal infections, difficulty conceiving, preventive screening, pregnancy care, or menopause concerns.
