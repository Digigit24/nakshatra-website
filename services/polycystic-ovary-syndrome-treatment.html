Overview

Care for hormonal balance, cycles, and fertility

Irregular periods, unwanted hair growth, acne, or difficulty conceiving can be signs of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome, a common hormonal disorder affecting ovarian function, fertility, and overall health. PCOS treatment at Nakshatra Clinic in Baner, Pune is designed to help restore hormonal balance, regulate menstrual cycles, improve fertility support, and manage PCOS symptoms effectively.

Treatment Options

PCOS treatment options

The original treatment options are preserved as responsive, readable cards without hiding content on mobile.

Lifestyle and Diet Management

A low-carbohydrate, nutrient-rich diet with regular exercise can improve insulin sensitivity, support ovulation, and reduce symptoms like irregular periods, excess hair growth, and acne.

Medications to Regulate Cycles

Combination birth control pills and progestin therapy may help restore regular periods, control acne and excess hair growth, and protect the uterine lining.

Fertility-Supporting Medications

Clomiphene, Letrozole, and Metformin may stimulate ovulation, improve insulin resistance, regulate blood sugar, and support pregnancy planning.

Excess Hair and Acne Care

Spironolactone, eflornithine cream, and electrolysis may help control unwanted hair growth and acne by addressing androgen effects.

Hormonal Therapy and Monitoring

Targeted hormonal therapy and regular monitoring help tailor care to each woman's hormonal profile, ovulation pattern, and fertility needs.

Insulin Resistance Management

Lifestyle changes, metformin, and dietary adjustments can help regulate insulin levels, support weight management, and reduce diabetes risk.

Advanced Fertility Support

Ovulation induction, timed intercourse guidance, IUI, and IVF can be considered for women with PCOS who are trying to conceive.

Holistic and Personalized Care

Customized PCOS plans combine lifestyle changes, medication, fertility support, and emotional guidance for symptom control and long-term health.
