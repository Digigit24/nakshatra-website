Overview

Relief and prevention for urinary tract infections

Urinary tract infections can affect the bladder, urethra, or kidneys and may cause burning urination, frequent urges, cloudy or foul-smelling urine, pelvic pain, or blood in urine. At Nakshatra Clinic in Baner, Pune, UTI care focuses on confirming the infection, selecting appropriate treatment, and giving practical guidance to prevent recurrence.

Symptoms & Risks

When UTI evaluation is important

Common symptoms and risk factors are grouped clearly so patients can seek timely care without waiting for symptoms to worsen.

Burning urination

Pain or burning while passing urine is one of the most common reasons to get urine testing and medical review.

Frequent urges

A repeated urge to urinate, passing small amounts, or nighttime frequency can suggest bladder irritation or infection.

Cloudy or foul-smelling urine

Urine color, smell, or visible blood may need urine routine and culture testing to guide treatment.

Pelvic pain

Lower abdominal or pelvic discomfort can occur with bladder infection and should be assessed if persistent.

Recurrent UTIs

Repeated infections may need culture-guided treatment, trigger review, imaging if needed, and prevention planning.

Risk factor review

Sexual activity, hygiene practices, contraceptive use, diabetes, pregnancy plans, and menopause can affect UTI risk.

Care Approach

How UTI treatment is planned

Care is focused on fast symptom control, accurate treatment, and long-term urinary health habits.

Urine testing

Urine routine, microscopy, and culture are advised according to symptoms, severity, and recurrence pattern.

Antibiotic therapy

Antibiotics are selected based on clinical need and culture findings when available, with instructions to complete the course.

Hydration and hygiene

Practical advice on water intake, hygiene, and bladder irritants supports recovery and prevention.

Follow-up monitoring

Review is planned when symptoms persist, infections recur, or additional testing is required.
