Overview

Understanding fibroids, symptoms, and treatment choices

Uterine fibroids, also called myomas, are noncancerous growths in or around the uterus. They can vary in size and number, and some women may have no symptoms. Others may experience heavy menstrual bleeding, pelvic pain, frequent urination, constipation, bloating, or fertility and pregnancy concerns. Treatment at Nakshatra Clinic is planned after careful diagnosis and discussion of your symptoms and reproductive goals.

Symptoms & Diagnosis

Signs that may need fibroid evaluation

The original fibroid concerns are organized into clear cards for easier scanning on desktop and mobile.

Heavy menstrual bleeding

Fibroids may cause heavy or prolonged periods and fatigue related to anemia.

Pelvic pain or pressure

Fibroids can create pelvic heaviness, discomfort, bloating, or pressure on nearby organs.

Urinary or bowel symptoms

Large fibroids may cause frequent urination, constipation, or abdominal swelling.

Ultrasound and MRI

Imaging helps understand fibroid size, number, and position before treatment decisions.

Hysteroscopy

Hysteroscopy may be advised when fibroids affect the uterine cavity or bleeding pattern.

Fertility review

Fibroids near the uterine cavity or tubes may need attention before conception planning.

Treatment Options

Fibroid care matched to symptoms and goals

Treatment can range from observation to medicines or procedures, depending on symptoms, fibroid profile, age, and fertility plans.

Observation

Small fibroids without symptoms may be monitored with regular follow-up and imaging.

Medication

Medicines may help reduce bleeding, pain, or anemia while planning longer-term treatment.

Myomectomy

Fibroid removal may be considered when uterus-sparing treatment is suitable or fertility goals matter.

UFE or RFA

Uterine fibroid embolization or radiofrequency ablation may be discussed for selected cases.

Hysterectomy

Hysterectomy may be discussed only when clinically suitable and family-building is complete.

Follow-up care

Follow-up supports symptom control, anemia correction, recurrence monitoring, and pregnancy planning.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Uterine fibroids, also called myomas, are noncancerous growths in or around the uterus. They may cause heavy bleeding, pelvic pain, bloating, or pressure symptoms.

Symptoms can include heavy menstrual bleeding, pelvic pain, frequent urination, constipation, bloating, and fatigue from anemia. Some women may have no symptoms.

Fibroids are typically diagnosed through pelvic examination, ultrasound, MRI, or hysteroscopy to understand their size and location.

Treatment options may include observation, medication, myomectomy, hysterectomy, uterine fibroid embolization, or radiofrequency ablation depending on the case.

Large fibroids or fibroids affecting the uterine cavity or fallopian tubes can interfere with conception or pregnancy. Early diagnosis helps plan fertility-conscious care.
